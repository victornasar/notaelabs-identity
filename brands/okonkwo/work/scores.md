# Okonkwo-Marchetti: route scores

Rubric from research §6 and §7.5. The sample leans toward large agencies, so these scores describe agency practice, not what this client needs. Type is scored within a free font library: the research's top tier (premium retail or custom type) is out of reach. The brief leaves the three competitors' identities undescribed, so "Distinct" is not scored and is left out of the totals.

## Route A: Pool

| Criterion | Score | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| One idea | 2 | Wordmark, monogram and the Lamplight color all come from `pool`; no pattern or layout device shown yet |
| Named convention | 2 | Rejects `spec-sheet` and `dark-and-glow` visibly: serif headlines on a light, warm page |
| Type | 2 | Hyphen redrawn as the pool (`type.wordmark.changes`); the change carries the idea but only shows from about 64 px |
| Equity | 2 | Names and the day and night pair evolved; Sodium kept but cut to 8% of a layout |
| Small sizes | 1 | Monogram O-M reads at 16 px, but the pool becomes a plain hyphen there, so the idea is lost (`review.at16`) |
| Product layer | 1 | Libre Franklin works at UI sizes and has a tabular figure style; Sodium sits close to an error red |
| Motion | 1 | The pool could fade in under the hyphen like a lamp switching on; the route doesn't state it |
| Distinct | – | Competitors not described in the brief |

**Strongest:** the calmest and most legible route, and the only one where the serif carries the "Warm" trait. **Weakest:** the idea disappears at small sizes and in the monogram's first read.

## Route B: Cut-off

| Criterion | Score | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| One idea | 2 | Lamp symbol, beam hyphen and hard-edged Sodium fields all come from `cone` and `cut-off`; a layout rule exists (`type.rules`) but no pattern is shown |
| Named convention | 2 | Dark-led without the glow: flat fields, no gradients, against `dark-and-glow` and `color-change` |
| Type | 3 | The beam replacing the hyphen is the same shape as the symbol's beam, so the wordmark's change recurs in the system |
| Equity | 3 | Sodium, the strongest asset, becomes 28% of every layout; names kept on one line |
| Small sizes | 3 | The lamp was drawn on a 24-unit grid with nothing under 3 units, and holds its silhouette on both grounds (`review.at16`) |
| Product layer | 1 | Chivo works at UI sizes; a dark-first palette with Sodium leaves little room for warning and error colors |
| Motion | 1 | A beam falling from the fitting is the obvious signature; the route doesn't state it |
| Distinct | – | Competitors not described in the brief |

**Strongest:** one shape, the beam, ties the wordmark, the symbol and the layouts together. **Weakest:** the darkest route, so it sits closest to the "gloomy" never; it depends on Sodium fields being used generously.

## Route C: Dusk

| Criterion | Score | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| One idea | 2 | The palette and the layout rule come from `kelvin-steps` and `dusk-pair`; the marks follow the idea only loosely |
| Named convention | 1 | Rejects `spec-sheet` in words, but a single grotesk family is the closest of the three to it |
| Type | 1 | Spacing, kerning and a hyphen redrawn at stem weight; no change carries the idea strongly |
| Equity | 2 | Keeps Edition 1's exact colors and earns Filament back as the middle kelvin step |
| Small sizes | 1 | The steps keep their silhouette at 16 px but read as a generic stack icon (`review.at16`) |
| Product layer | 1 | One grotesk family suits UI; Filament overlaps a warning yellow and Sodium an error red |
| Motion | 1 | The steps could light in kelvin order; the route doesn't state it |
| Distinct | – | Competitors not described in the brief |

**Strongest:** the most complete color system, keeping all of Edition 1's color equity. **Weakest:** the marks are the weakest of the three; the idea lives only in color.

## Summary

Route B scores highest (15 of 21), then A (11) and C (9). The criterion where each could gain most:
- **A:** small sizes. Give the monogram a pool that survives at 16 px.
- **B:** motion. State the beam as the signature motion and use it for loading.
- **C:** type. Carry the step into the letters rather than only the hyphen.
