# Okonkwo-Marchetti: strategy

Drawn from the approved brief (`brief.md`). The routes come after this is approved, and every visual choice in them has to cite a decision here.

## Concept

**Idea.** Good night light comes in measured pools with honest darkness between them, and that rhythm is the walk home.

**Source.** A lit street seen from above at night: lamp, a pool of light on the pavement, a dark interval, the next lamp. Sodium orange is its most familiar color, and the studio's Edition 1 already owns it.

This idea answers all three "nevers". It isn't spectacular, because a pool is modest, fixed and warm. It isn't technical, because it describes what a person on the street experiences, not the fitting. It isn't gloomy, because the darkness is measured and part of the design, not a black backdrop for photographs. It is also the studio's actual method: decide where light lands, and decide where it doesn't.

**Primitives.** Every mark, device and layout is built from these:

| id | Shape | From the idea |
|---|---|---|
| `pool` | A flat ellipse, about 3:1, or the half of it above its long axis | The light a lamp lays on the ground |
| `cone` | A trapezoid, narrow at the top and widening downward at a fixed angle | A beam leaving a fitting |
| `interval` | An even, measured gap between repeated pools, equal to one pool's width | The dark between lamps; the rhythm of the walk |
| `cut-off` | One hard horizontal edge where light stops | A glare-free fitting's cut-off line |
| `kelvin-steps` | Bands of color stepping from sodium orange (about 1800 K) to warm white (about 3000 K) | Color temperature, the one number every lighting decision names |
| `dusk-pair` | Two grounds, pale stone by day and basalt by night; the same layout flips at sunset | The same building before and after sunset (evolved from Edition 1) |

## Category

**Architectural lighting design.** Competitors from the brief: Speirs Major, Arup Lighting, L'Observatoire International. Their identities, from brief round 2 (read from their sites' text, not seen):

| Competitor | Identity |
|---|---|
| Speirs Major | The name as plain capitals, "light architecture" as a lowercase descriptor; a sparse, project-led site |
| Arup Lighting | A service line inside Arup's corporate brand and red logo; full-width project photography, night scenes included |
| L'Observatoire International | A wordmark with a symbol and the line "Lighting the World"; offices listed by city |

The conventions are the category's, taken from the brief's "never" answers, and none is attributed to a firm.

| id | Convention | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `color-change` | Facades that cycle through colors, with dynamic RGB light as the selling point | **Reject** | "Spectacular" is a never; the studio's light stays put and stays warm |
| `dark-and-glow` | Black websites and portfolios with glowing night photographs of lit buildings | **Reject** | "Gloomy" is a never; it treats darkness as a backdrop, not as designed |
| `spec-sheet` | Technical identities: engineering sans, cool greys, product-catalog grids | **Reject** | "Technical" is a never; the audience buys judgment, not fittings |
| `night-photography` | Leading with photographs of finished projects at night | **Keep** | The work is seen at night and in photographs; the identity frames the photos instead of competing with them |

## Equity

**Refresh.** Edition 1 exists, and the brief keeps more than it drops.

| id | Asset | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `names` | *Okonkwo-Marchetti* on one line, joined by the hyphen | **Keep** | The partnership is the studio |
| `descriptor` | "Architectural Lighting Studio" as part of the name | **Evolve** | Moves out of the mark into a separate descriptor, so the mark can hold at small sizes |
| `sodium` | Sodium, the streetlight orange | **Keep** | The strongest thing in Edition 1, and the source of the idea |
| `dusk-pair` | Limestone and Basalt, the day and night pair | **Evolve** | Becomes the `dusk-pair` primitive: the whole layout flips at sunset |
| `filament-mercury` | Filament yellow and Mercury teal | **Drop** | Unless a route earns them as a `kelvin-steps` band |
| `voice` | The voice traits Warm and Exacting | **Keep** | They describe the studio; the visual system has to match them |
| `wordmark-e1` | Edition 1's wordmark, Bricolage Grotesque set as it comes | **Drop** | A rejected font, typed out, with no changes |

## What the routes must do

- Each route leads with different primitives, takes a different type category, and has a different value structure (light-led, dark-led, or both through `dusk-pair`).
- Sodium appears in every route, because the brief keeps it. How much of each layout it takes is a route decision.
- No route uses color cycling, glow effects or a black-first site without a reason drawn from the idea.
