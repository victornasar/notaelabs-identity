# Notae Labs: brief

Notae has an earlier identity (Edition 1, in `brands/notae/brand.ts`), so this is a refresh or a replacement, and the interview has to settle what Edition 1 owns. Its fonts (Source Serif 4, IBM Plex Sans) are now rejected and are not carried over.

## Brief so far

1. Category: open. Edition 1 says a studio that "turns a founder's brief into a living brand site". The product now also generates the identity.
2. Three competitors: open.
3. Audience: proposed from Edition 1: a founder with a product and no usable identity; a designer, an engineer or an agent implements from the site afterward. Not confirmed.
4. Equity: open. Edition 1 has the name, the Paper/Ink/Signal palette, a serif voice, the registration-mark symbol, and the voice traits Exact, Plain, Calm.
5. Never: proposed from Edition 1: hype, mystique, decoration that doesn't carry a decision. No example brands yet.
6. Budget tier: open.
7. Deadline: open.

## Round 1

1. **[Category]** What is Notae Labs, in the words a buyer would use: a studio that sells identities, software a client uses on their own, or a studio whose deliverable is software (the identity plus the tools that keep it in use)? And what is it not?
   *Proposed:* the third. The research found the best studios starting to ship tools as part of an identity (ElevenLabs, Cohere), and called it Notae's real opening. Not a brand-guidelines CMS.

   *Proposed:* one studio at the buyer's budget, one guidelines platform (Frontify, Corebook or Brandpad), and the do-it-yourself option (a freelancer with a Figma template). Replace these with the names you actually lose deals to.

3. **[Audience]** Is Edition 1's buyer still right: a founder whose company has a product but no identity the team can use? What stage (pre-seed, seed, Series A), how big a team, and in what kind of company: AI, dev tools, fintech, something else?
   *Proposed:* seed to Series A software companies, 5–40 people, where the founder signs and a designer or engineer builds.
   **Answer:**

4. **[Equity]** Which parts of Edition 1 do you want to keep, evolve or drop? For each, how attached are you, and has anyone outside seen it?
   - the name *Notae* (Latin for notes or marks) and *Labs*
   - the registration-mark symbol (circle and cross)
   - the Paper / Ink / Signal-blue palette
   - a serif as the main voice
   - the voice traits Exact, Plain, Calm
   *Proposed:* keep the name and the voice traits; drop the symbol and the blue. The research reads the symbol as a stock "precision" crosshair (a gunsight in the lockup) and the palette as the most default one in software.
